How It Works

No API keys. No accounts. Just pay and use.

1

Pay with USDC

Send USDC to the AgentOS treasury wallet on Solana. Amount depends on the service.

2

Call the API

Include the Solana transaction signature in the X-Payment header. That's your auth.

3

Service Provisioned

AgentOS verifies payment on-chain and provisions your resource instantly. Done. ⚡

# Provision a phone number for your agent
curl -X POST https://api.agentos.dev/phone/numbers \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-Payment: <solana-tx-signature>" \
  -d '{"country": "US"}'
